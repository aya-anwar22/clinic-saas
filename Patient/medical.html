Pregnancy or Active Lactation Strict Contraindication

Laser optical radiation and vascular micro-thermolysis protocols are strictly contraindicated during pregnancy and breastfeeding stages.

Photosensitizing Medications & Retinoids High Precaution

Includes Isotretinoin (Accutane) within past 6 months, oral Doxycycline, Tetracyclines, topical Retin-A / Tretinoin, or St. John's Wort.

Keloid or Hypertrophic Scarring History

Abnormal dermal fibroblastic proliferation following mechanical trauma or laser heating requires conservative sub-ablative pulse regimes.

Active Dermal Lesions or Cold Sores (HSV)

Active herpes simplex virus, open abrasions, dermatitis flares, or fungal manifestations in the scheduled treatment anatomical site.

Implanted Electronic Hardware & Pacemakers

Cardiac pacemakers, subcutaneous ICDs, or internal metallic implants in proximity to the target optical zone.

Topical Anesthetic Sensitivity (Lidocaine / Tetracaine)

Documented hypersensitivity or anaphylactoid response to ester or amide compound local numbing preparations.
